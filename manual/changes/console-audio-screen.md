---
title: Add an Audio screen under the controller scheme
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets:
- type: key
  id: ControlScheme
  effect: changed
credit: [gibbo101]
---

Under the `Controller` control scheme the options screen and the in-game menu offer an Audio screen with the music, sound, and voice volumes, applied as they step; in play it also offers shuffle, repeat, a Now Playing row, a Track row that steps through the songs by name and plays the chosen one on accept, and Stop Music, as the sound controls dialog does. Accept keeps the volumes and saves them; back restores them. The `KeyboardMouse` dialogs are unchanged.
