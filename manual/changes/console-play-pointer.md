Under the `Controller` control scheme, in play the left stick and the d-pad move the pointer, a shoulder button speeds it, and the accept and back buttons act as the left and right mouse buttons at the pointer, so selecting, ordering, deploying, placing, band boxing, and cancelling work as they do with a mouse. The other buttons run the game's own commands: the third face button cycles the sidebar's repair, sell, power, and waypoint modes, the stick clicks deploy and centre on the base, the right trigger scatters or, with the right shoulder, guards, the right shoulder with back rebuilds the last building, the left trigger or left shoulder with a face button makes or selects a team, the right shoulder with the left shoulder or trigger holds the force fire or force move key, and the view button allies with the selected unit's owner. The fourth face button takes the controller to the sidebar, where a highlight moves between the cameos and the mode buttons and the accept button builds, places, or presses what it marks. The keyboard scheme is unchanged.
