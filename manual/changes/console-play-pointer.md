Under the `Controller` control scheme, in play the left stick and the d-pad move the pointer, which starts a scenario mid-map, the right shoulder speeds it, and the accept and back buttons act as the left and right mouse buttons at the pointer, so selecting, ordering, deploying, placing, band boxing, and canceling work as they do with a mouse. Accept held still on one of the player's units selects every unit of its type on screen and then across the map; held still on the ground it selects every combat unit on screen and then across the map. The other buttons run the game's own commands: the third face button cycles the sidebar's repair, sell, power, and waypoint modes, passing over a mode the game refuses; the stick clicks deploy and center on the base; the right trigger scatters, or in waypoint mode takes back the last waypoint, and with the right shoulder guards; the left trigger or left shoulder with a face button makes or selects a team; the right shoulder with the left shoulder or trigger force fires or force moves at the pointer; and the view button allies with the selected unit's owner. The keyboard scheme is unchanged.
