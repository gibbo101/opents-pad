category: feature

- type: key
  id: ControlScheme
  effect: changed
credit: [gibbo101]

Under the `Controller` control scheme, in play the left stick and the d-pad move the pointer, a shoulder button speeds it, and the accept and back buttons act as the left and right mouse buttons at the pointer, so selecting, ordering, deploying, placing, band boxing, and cancelling work as they do with a mouse. The keyboard scheme is unchanged.
