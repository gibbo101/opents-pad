Under the `Controller` control scheme, in play the left stick and the d-pad move the pointer, which starts a scenario mid-map, the right shoulder speeds it, and the accept and back buttons act as the left and right mouse buttons at the pointer, so selecting, ordering, deploying, placing, band boxing, and canceling work as they do with a mouse. A second tap of accept on one of the player's units selects every unit of its type on screen and a third tap every unit of its type on the map; tapped twice on the ground it selects every combat unit on screen, and a third tap every combat unit on the map. The other buttons run the game's own commands: the third face button cycles the sidebar's repair, sell, power, and waypoint modes, passing over a mode the game refuses; the stick clicks deploy and center on the base; the right trigger scatters, or in waypoint mode takes back the last waypoint, and with the right shoulder guards; the left trigger or left shoulder with a face button makes or selects a team; the right shoulder with the left shoulder or trigger force fires or force moves at the pointer; and the view button centers the view on the last radar event in a solo game or allies with the selected unit's owner in a network game. Units in a team wear the glyph of the button that made them in place of the number. The keyboard scheme is unchanged.
